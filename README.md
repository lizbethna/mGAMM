# mGAMM
A Bayesian generalized additive mixed model under monotone constraints

Lizbeth Naranjo-Albarrán (1), Patricio Maturana-Russel (2,3), and Víctor Miranda-Soberanis (2)

(1) Departamento de Matemáticas, Facultad de Ciencias, Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México, Mexico; 
(2) Department of Mathematical Sciences, Auckland University of Technology, New Zealand; 
(3) Department of Statistics, University of Auckland, New Zealand

ABSTRACT
In many practical settings involving longitudinal data, the response variable is ex-
pected to follow specific shape constraints, such as monotonic trends—for example,
when patients’ health is anticipated to decline over time. To address this, we pro-
pose a novel class of monotonic generalized additive models with mixed components
(mGAMMs) for longitudinal data. Within a Bayesian framework, monotonicity is
enforced through smoothing functions and penalized splines that capture increasing
or decreasing patterns, together with constraints on the prior distribution parame-
ters. The approach is illustrated using both simulated data and a real health science
dataset, highlighting its computational advantages as well as potential limitations.
The proposed methods show improved fitting performance, particularly for small
datasets.

KEYWORDS
Bayesian statistics; Generalized additive models; Integrated penalized splines;
Longitudinal data; Monotone constraints; Random effects
